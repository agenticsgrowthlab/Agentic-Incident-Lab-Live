# Payment Incident Customer Impact Playbook
Type: Customer control

Count affected payment instructions, distinct customers, repeat attempts, support contacts, and abandoned journeys separately. Multiple attempts from one customer must not be reported as multiple affected customers. Proactive messaging should tell customers whether to wait, retry, or contact support and must avoid encouraging additional retries during a retry storm.

Prioritize customers facing time-sensitive obligations and identify any fees or downstream consequences created by delay. Support teams need a single current incident message and a reconciliation path.
